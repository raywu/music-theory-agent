# Visualization

Plain text is the guaranteed baseline.

Use visualization when the learner's obstacle is spatial or relational rather than merely factual. Good uses include semitone rulers, interval ladders, chord-tone alignment, scale-degree rows, fifth chains, and voice-leading maps.

Prefer the smallest diagram that reveals the relationship. Fade diagrams when they begin to reveal answers the learner should retrieve.

Rich notation, keyboards, audio, and interactive widgets are progressive enhancement. Never make them required to continue.
