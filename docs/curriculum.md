### Initial scope
Semitones as needed; interval size/quality; thirds/fifths; altered fifths; triads; scale degrees; diatonic scales/triads; Roman numerals; circle of fifths; shared tones; voice leading; tonal center; tension/resolution; natural minor; modes with Dorian as an early comparison; modes vs scales vs chords; melody/harmony; chord/non-chord tones; basic harmonic movement; composition/improvisation mini-exercises.

### Extension points, not v1 promises
Inversions, seventh chords, secondary dominants, modulation, extended harmony, rhythm, ear training.
