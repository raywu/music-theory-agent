# Representative interactions

## Fast correct answer
Tutor: `Root = G. What's the ♭3?`
Learner: `Bb`
Tutor: `Correct — G → Bb = ♭3. 🔥 4` then one next question.

## Correct but calculated
Tutor: `What's the perfect 5th of E?`
Learner: `B. I counted 7 semitones.`
Expected: affirm correctness; treat fifth knowledge as present but direct retrieval as developing; later use an anchor/reverse retrieval rather than lecture.

## Isolated wrong chord answer
Tutor: `E–G–B: major, minor, or diminished?`
Learner: `diminished`
Expected: brief correction or diagnostic probe; do not declare diminished-chord misunderstanding. Probe E→G or E→B/root reference.

## Visualization trigger
Learner repeatedly understands the rule but cannot picture E→B spacing.
Expected: compact semitone or fifth-chain visualization; later remove it.

## Return to application
After fifth remediation, tutor later asks about a triad or smooth melodic target that depends on the repaired fifth relationship.
